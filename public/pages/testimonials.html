Testimonials

Caz is an amazing, supportive and empathetic therapist. Over our time together, we have built a relationship that is a balance of acknowledgement and exploration of experiences. I have learnt so much about myself and developed much greater self awareness through our work together. Caz is able to interpret my experiences in a way I hadn't thought of them, helping me discover more about myself, her interpretations are not pushy or one sided, there is always room to refine them together to get to a picture, an image or a conclusion that really resonates with me.

I have a very logical mind, I like facts, results and other such tangible things, something that is very difficult to get from therapy. Caz is able to work with me to support that side of me that "wants results" but also help me to see the progress I am making even if it cannot be quantified. I now enjoy the process of working on myself and have even become comfortable with the feeling of unknown and "work in progress". Something like enjoying the journey rather than wanting to reach the destination.
